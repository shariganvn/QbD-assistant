## Observations

- Direct compression showed poorer flow than wet granulation (Carr index 24%).
- Dissolution is slower than F-01 but still meets specification.
- Hardness range is wider — compression force control is more critical.
- Higher disintegrant level (5%) compensates for lack of granule bonding.
- Content uniformity AV is higher (11.2 vs 6.8), indicating mixing is less homogeneous.
- Simplified process (fewer steps) reduces manufacturing time by ~40%.
