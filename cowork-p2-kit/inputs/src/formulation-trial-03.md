## Observations

- Higher dose (10 mg) gives better content uniformity (AV 5.4) due to lower relative potency variation.
- Dissolution is fastest among all three formulations — higher drug load creates more porous granules.
- Hardness is highest (72 N) — good mechanical stability.
- Binder level increased (8 mg vs 5 mg) to accommodate higher drug loading.
- Yellow coating distinguishes 10 mg from 5 mg (white) for patient safety.
