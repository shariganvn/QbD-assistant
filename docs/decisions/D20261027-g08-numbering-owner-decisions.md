2. **Source of the ACTD numbering: the draft Phụ lục I (2025).** The numbering is checked against that draft. If the annex is issued with other numbers, the map is checked again; item 15 of the checklist keeps saying it waits for the issued annex.
3. **NCE and biotech products: not applicable.** ACTD P 2.1 (development studies) applies to them only, so the map has no entry for it and a generic draft has no such section. This matches the scope decision (combinations, NCE and biologics are outside the SOP).
4. **ACTD numbers are printed.** For the domestic and both markets every mapped heading shows `(ACTD Pn.n)` after the CTD number, and the section status table does too. The EU page shows none. The page check fails when a number is missing.
